Generate one opaque 2560×1440 landscape raster background. A continuous near-black #0b0c0e field with extremely fine, clean, round indigo-blue #2752f5 and soft periwinkle dots arranged on a precise regular straight raster. At a 1440px display width dots should read about 1–2px across on a 4–5px grid. Use gradual opacity and subtle dot-size/density changes to form a very restrained broad tonal bloom strongest toward the far-right and lower edge. Preserve generous calm near-black reading space across the left and upper-middle. Dots must look visibly round and intentional, like exceptionally fine screen-printing, not stars, dust, film grain, noise, perspective mesh, swirls, circles, clouds, fog, lines or coarse polka dots. Dark gaps between dots remain near-black; no bright blue continuous wash. Smooth tonal transitions with no hard seams or abrupt shapes. The dots must remain crisp at native size while the overall pattern remains quiet enough behind white UI text.

This asset will be used via cover crops on a website hero and GitHub banner sizes 1280×640 and 1280×1046. Keep the visual useful across these crops. Straight-on flat 2D printed material, no lighting or perspective effects. No text, no branding, no logo, no objects, no controls, no screenshots, no borders, no watermark. One background asset only.
